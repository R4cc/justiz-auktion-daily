# Economy balance audit — 23 October 2026

Source: /home/lxadm/Downloads/auction-archive.json; 1976 lots. Case figures average exact outcome distributions over 30 UTC rotations and uniform market indexes 50, 100 and 150. These are reproducible reference-value scenarios, not production telemetry or guaranteed sale proceeds. No player data is written.

The benchmark is a mixed casual economy: Daily income should support participation; trade and stores should reward capital, stock and time; theft should escalate in risk rather than replace ordinary income. The 20% cap applies to every numerical adjustment in this pass. Larger structural loopholes remain deferred.

## Deferred structural issues and limits

See [documented economy loopholes](economy-loopholes.md) for unpaid theft compensation, repeat secondary-case Prestige subsidies, and profitable player-to-player Prestige wash trades. They are deliberately not patched under this pass's universal 20% limit. Ordinary receipt idempotency does not prevent those separate economic loops. Their existence limits claims about aggregate currency conservation.

This report does not model player cooperation, real liquidation rates, turnover, borrowing behavior or retention. Market frenzy and manual event timing can change outcomes after purchase. Historical archive snapshots and synthetic scenarios are references; tune further only with measured production behavior and separately approved structural fixes.
